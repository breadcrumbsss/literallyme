# Quotes Reference

Lines from *Drive* (2011) and where to find them. Use them sparingly. A well-placed line lands better than a constant one.

## The rules

Driver lays out how he works. This is the sigma blueprint.

> **Driver:** If I drive for you, you get your money. You tell me where we start, where we're going, where we're going afterwards. I give you five minutes when we get there. Anything happens in that five minutes, and I'm yours. No matter what. Anything a minute on either side of that, and you're on your own. I don't sit in while you're running it down. I don't carry a gun. I drive.

## The opening monologue

> **Driver:** There's a hundred-thousand streets in this city. You don't need to know the route. You give me a time and a place, I give you a five minute window. Anything happens in that five minutes, and I'm yours. No matter what. Anything happens a minute either side of that, and you're on your own. Do you understand? Good. And you won't be able to reach me on this phone again.
